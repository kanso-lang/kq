# measured

Regenerated by CI on every commit — every figure here is an allocator
counter, deterministic to the byte on any machine. The wall-clock and
hardware-counter tables in README.md are a dated sitting on a quiet box and
are moved by hand; bench/numbers_gate.kso refuses to let them outlive the
compiler they were measured against.

`ratio` is the 2.1 MB fixture over the 188 KB one, ten times the document.
Work that is linear in the input lands near 10.
